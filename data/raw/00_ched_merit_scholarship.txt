# CHED Merit Scholarship Program (CMSP) Guidelines

Source: Commission on Higher Education (CHED) Official Guidelines
Reference: https://legacy.ched.gov.ph/merit-scholarship/

Overview:
The CHED Merit Scholarship Program (CMSP) is a competitive government scholarship that provides financial assistance to academically talented and deserving Filipino students pursuing undergraduate degrees in CHED-priority programs.

Eligibility Requirements:
- Must be a Filipino citizen.
- Must be a graduating senior high school student or high school graduate with a minimum General Weighted Average (GWA) of 93% or its equivalent.
- Combined annual gross income of parents or legal guardian must not exceed PHP 500,000.
- In cases where the income exceeds PHP 500,000, an applicant may still qualify if there are special circumstances (such as multiple siblings enrolled in college or high medical expenses), subject to verification.
- Must secure admission in State Universities and Colleges (SUCs), Local Universities and Colleges (LUCs) with Institutional Recognition / Certificate of Program Compliance (COPC), or CHED-recognized Private Higher Education Institutions (PHEIs).

Documentary Requirements:
- Accomplished Online Application Form / Annex A
- Birth Certificate (PSA or NSO authenticated copy)
- Certified true copy of Grade 12 School Form 9 (SF9) / Form 138 (High School Report Card)
- Proof of income (any one applicable):
  * Latest Income Tax Return (ITR) of parents/guardian
  * BIR Certificate of Tax Exemption / Non-Filer Certificate
  * DSWD Certificate of Indigence or Case Study Report
  * Latest employment contract or proof of income for Overseas Filipino Workers (OFWs) and seafarers
- Special group documents if applicable:
  * Person with Disability (PWD) identification card
  * Solo Parent ID from City/Municipal Social Welfare and Development Office
  * Indigenous Peoples Certification from the National Commission on Indigenous Peoples (NCIP)

Coverage and Financial Benefits:
- Covers Tuition and Other School Fees (TOSF) for private HEI slots or free tuition subsidy under Republic Act 10931 for SUCs/LUCs.
- Provides a regular monthly/semester stipend released directly to scholars or through Higher Education Institutions.
- Includes a dedicated book allowance and connectivity/learning materials allowance per semester until the completion of the degree program.

Application Process & How to Apply:
- Prepare and scan clear digital copies (PDF or high-res images) of all required documentary requirements.
- Submit applications online through the official CHED Regional Office (CHEDRO) portal corresponding to the applicant's province or region of residence.
- Wait for the evaluation and publication of the official list of qualified CMSP awardees by your respective CHED Regional Office.
- Official Information Portal: https://legacy.ched.gov.ph/merit-scholarship/
