Title: CHED Merit Scholarship Program (CMSP)
Overview: The CHED Merit Scholarship Program provides financial assistance to academically talented and deserving Filipino students.
Eligibility Requirements:
- Must be a Filipino citizen.
- Must be a senior high school graduate or graduating student with a minimum General Weighted Average (GWA) of 93% or its equivalent.
- Combined annual gross income of parents or legal guardian must not exceed PHP 500,000.

Documentary Requirements:
- Accomplished Online Application Form
- Certified true copy of Form 138 (High School Report Card)
- Proof of income (Latest Income Tax Return, Certificate of Tax Exemption, DSWD Certificate of Indigence, or OFW contract)
- Birth Certificate (PSA/NSO)
- Special group documents if applicable (e.g., PWD ID, Solo Parent ID, Indigenous Peoples Certification from NCIP)

Coverage and Benefits:
- Covers tuition and other school fees (TOSF).
- Provides a stipend released directly to scholars or through Higher Education Institutions (HEIs).
- Includes a book and connectivity allowance per semester until the completion of the degree program.

Application Process:
- Prepare and scan clear copies of all documentary requirements.
- Submit applications online to the respective CHED Regional Office (CHEDRO) based on the applicant's point of origin.
- Reference website: https://legacy.ched.gov.ph/merit-scholarship/
